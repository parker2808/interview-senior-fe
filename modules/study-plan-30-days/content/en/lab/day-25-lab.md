# Day 25 — Lab

> Timebox 45-60 minutes. Interview drill, not a product build.

## What they will ask

Introduce yourself. What was your biggest impact? What makes someone senior — and how do you demonstrate it without a title speech?

## What you will produce

- Write and record a 60-90 second self-intro that frames Vue depth, frontend fundamentals, and active React/Next growth honestly.
- Pick one impact story and rewrite it in STAR with measurable results.

## How a senior works this

- **Decision:** Spoken intro first, then one STAR impact from a system you shipped. Seniority is judgment + ownership + leverage, not years.
- **Constraint:** Voice recording + STAR write-up. Not a Vue spike, not a slide deck.
- **Failure mode:** A resume recitation. Impact with no number. ‘I am passionate about clean code.’ Hiding the React gap or overselling it.
- **Measure:** A spoken self-intro exists. One impact story is in STAR form and measurable.
- **Tradeoff:** Depth vs breadth in the intro. Lead with Vue production depth; put React in the growth sentence so they do not discover the gap as a surprise.
- **Production gotcha:** Stories that need a teammate to confirm the number. ‘We’ with no ‘I’. Timed intros that run 3 minutes because you added a second project.

## Done when

- A spoken self-intro now exists, not just bullet notes.
- One impact story is in STAR form and measurable.
- Weak-topic algo drill #1 is complete.
- You can explain one trade-off from today in under 2 minutes (decision → constraint → failure → measure).
